### 🚀 What we build

- 🤖 **AI agents for one job in your business**: legal research, accounting, lead generation, SEO, document processing
- 🩺 **Fixes for AI assistants that give wrong answers**: find where it breaks, fix it, prove it with evals
- 🏗️ **Prototype to production**: tracing, guardrails, cost per answer, model-agnostic design
- 🖥️ **Self-hosted LLMs** when your data can't leave your servers (vLLM, SGLang, LoRA/QLoRA)
- 🔌 **MCP servers** that plug our agents into your dev tools and workflows
